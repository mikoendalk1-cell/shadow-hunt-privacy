This Privacy Policy explains how Shadow Hunt ("the Game," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you play the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Because Shadow Hunt includes online multiplayer, we collect the following to make matchmaking and gameplay work:

  • Device identifiers (such as a randomly generated player/device ID) used to connect you to multiplayer matches
  • In-game username or nickname you choose, shown to other players during matches
  • Approximate connection/network information (such as IP address) used only for matchmaking and to prevent abuse/cheating
  • Basic gameplay data (matches played, wins/losses) to support game features

We do not collect your real name, email address, or precise location unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, by contacting us for support).

2. How We Use Information

  • To connect you with other players in online matches
  • To maintain game functionality and prevent cheating or abuse
  • To improve gameplay and fix bugs

3. Advertising

Shadow Hunt does not currently display third-party advertisements or use advertising SDKs.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data may be shared only with backend service providers (such as multiplayer server hosting) strictly to operate the Game's online features.

5. Children's Privacy

Shadow Hunt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

6. Data Retention

Multiplayer session and matchmaking data is retained only as long as needed to operate the Game's online features, and may be deleted periodically.

7. Your Choices

You can stop all data collection related to online play by not using the multiplayer features of the Game.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
